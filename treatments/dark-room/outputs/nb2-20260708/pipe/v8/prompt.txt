An extreme macro still-life close-up in the dark. A single clear glass oil-burner — a round glass bulb at one end connected to a long straight glass stem — rests horizontally in the frame. The round glass bulb glows intensely hot, red and orange, blazing bright from within, with a thin curl of pale smoke rising slowly from it. A small warm orange flame flickers up just beneath the round bulb, gently heating the glass. Razor-sharp macro focus on the glowing red-hot bulb and the little flame, delicate reflections running along the clear glass stem; everything lit only by the red-hot glass and the flame, warm orange light against deep, total black. No people anywhere, only the glowing glass and the flame.

Sensory 35mm film still — shallow depth of field, fine film grain, warm orange light. In the style of a 1990s golden-era hip hop music video crossed with Abel Ferrara's King of New York (1990): 35mm motion picture film, natural Kodak color, deep shadows, glossy photographic print finish.
