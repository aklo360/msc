## Synopsis

Dark Room is a music video about success being haunted by survival. Mal begins in a clean, expensive present-day world inside a luxury Manhattan condo. As the song turns, the space is invaded by flashes of the trap-house past: dirty couch, stained table, scales, baggies, smoke, harsh light, door pressure, and memories that refuse to stay buried.

The video moves through three core scenes. First is the two-part master scene: Mal's glossy present-day luxury world interwoven with the trap-house flashback. Second is the literal dark-room void: a black performance space with one hanging bulb where props, extras, and montage details mutate around him with Requiem for a Dream-inspired rhythm. Third is Star and Mal's B-story: city movement over the Williamsburg Bridge, film development, and printed photographs that transform into the other scenes until the whole video folds back into itself.

Polaroid-style freeze frames appear throughout, turning key moments into physical photographs. By the end, the dark room means the room they survived, the place where photos develop, and the mental space that still follows them into success.

## Line-by-Line Timecode Treatment

### 00:00.000-00:15.080 - Penthouse Terrace / Morning Loop

Mal sits on the penthouse terrace in the MSC silk robe, sunrise opening over Manhattan below him. The world is pristine: cigar smoke, untouched French omelette, mimosa glass, polished surfaces, city quiet.

He performs inwardly, not to camera, as if the luxury is moving around him but he is mentally stuck. He takes a sip, sets the mimosa beside the omelette, rises, and walks inside toward the bedroom. The first feeling is expensive stillness with pressure underneath.

### 00:15.080-00:18.380 - Bedroom Ritual / Weapon As Routine

Inside the bedroom, Mal continues the ritual without breaking into camera performance. He puts on his watch, then his shoes, then reaches for the gun.

The gun goes into his waistband with the same calm precision as the watch and shoes. Success, presentation, and survival all belong to the same morning routine.

### 00:18.380-00:21.780 - Stairs / Mentor Echo

Mal exits the bedroom and starts down the stairs. The movement is slow and elegant: close details of robe fabric, jewelry, shoes, handrail, polished wood.

The performance still feels like he is talking to himself. The luxury setting becomes a stage for the old voice in his head, pushing him not to waste what he has.

### 00:21.780-00:25.000 - Door Pause / Cake Flash

At the ground floor, Mal reaches the front door. His hand goes to the knob, then stops.

He looks back over his shoulder at the foyer: plastic-covered couch, pristine antique coffee table, perfect stillness. On the cake/status cue, we flash to blue hundreds on marble being sliced open like cake, then snap back to the foyer as the first flashback begins.

### 00:25.000-00:32.480 - First Hook / Couch Portal

The couch and coffee table become the portal between the luxury condo and the trap house of the past. The plastic-covered luxury couch turns into a dirty beat-up couch; the antique coffee table becomes a stained work table.

Past Mal appears on the couch, performing the hook but still not to camera. He looks straight off, locked into the room, with scales, baggies, coke, cash, wrappers, sticky glass, ash, and stains spread across the table.

### 00:32.480-00:35.860 - Table Work / Dark-Room Void

The condo melts away until only the couch and coffee table still connect the two worlds. Everything else falls into blackness.

The true dark-room void takes over: pure black, one hanging bulb, Mal isolated. Then the abstract void gives way to the narrative past, with people around the table moving through the room's business.

### 00:35.860-00:39.260 - Blinds / Daylight Swallowed

Mal is now in the trap-house bedroom, rapping toward the window instead of camera. He puts his fingers through the blinds and opens them slightly.

A hard beam of daylight enters the room, cutting through dust and smoke. Instead of saving him, the light gets swallowed by the room's darkness until the image dips toward black.

### 00:39.260-00:43.040 - Light Switch / Floor Scatter

At pure black, the hanging bulb flicks on in close-up. Mal hits a switch.

The floor becomes visible for a split second: grime, movement, and cockroaches scattering away from the light. We snap back to the wide dark-room setup with Mal under the bulb, now performing directly into the overhead world.

### 00:43.040-00:46.320 - Door Pressure / No Rest

The room becomes restless. A knock hits the door. Another body shifts near the frame edge. Someone waits outside.

Mal stays on the couch/table axis, tired but alert. The room never lets him settle; every sound becomes a check, every knock becomes a decision.

### 00:46.320-00:48.980 - Body Detail / Liquor And Sweat

The montage moves close: sweat on skin, smoke near his mouth, a collar, a glass, the shine of liquor, the texture of the room clinging to his body.

The frame should feel sensory and uncomfortable, like the environment is coming out through him.

### 00:48.980-00:53.060 - Serving / Watching For Law

The trap-house room becomes a workplace. Cash moves forward. A hand reaches in. Product and money exchange without explaining mechanics.

Mal's eyes cut to the door before he completes the move. The rhythm is listen, look, move, serve, reset.

### 00:53.060-00:55.640 - Water Survival Transition

Water becomes the transition out of the room. A glass, sink, or spill on the table expands into a street reflection.

Mal's reflection breaks across wet pavement. The image turns predatory and survival-driven, as if being thrown into danger forced him to become more dangerous.

### 00:55.640-00:59.700 - Winter Street / Exposed Need

The room opens into a cold exterior memory. Winter blue light, wet sidewalk, underdressed people moving through the block, breath visible in the air.

The trap-house pressure is no longer contained indoors. The need follows people outside, making the whole street feel like an extension of the room.

### 00:59.700-01:03.380 - Smile / Pain / Rain

Mal appears in fragments: reflected in wet pavement, seen through rain-streaked glass, doubled in the apartment window, then back in the dark room.

He smiles, but the image undercuts it. The rain, reflection, and window doubles make the smile feel like armor over pain.

### 01:03.380-01:06.700 - Seizure Flash

A quick memory flash interrupts the rhythm: someone buckles, hands rush in, a small object hits the floor.

Mal registers it for a fraction of a second before the edit pulls away. The moment lands as trauma, not exposition.

### 01:06.700-01:12.380 - Hook Return / Static Overhead Fisheye

The hook returns in the overhead dark-room setup. Mal is alone under the bulb at first, rapping up into camera.

The shot stays locked and malleable. Time starts to skip around him.

### 01:12.380-01:15.740 - Extras Enter / Table Appears

The overhead setup changes in jumps. One person appears, then three bodies are suddenly around him.

The table appears under the bulb, then disappears, then returns messier. Mal stays centered while the room mutates around him.

### 01:15.740-01:19.140 - Light Outside / Dark Inside Refrain

Brief flashes of the luxury apartment interrupt the dark room: the omelette, mimosa glass, robe pattern, front door knob, antique table.

Each clean detail gets darkened by the memory overlay. The present tries to return, but the dark room keeps winning.

### 01:19.140-01:22.960 - Club Memory / Smoke Introduction

The B-story energy begins with a club memory. Smoke, low light, packed bodies, and a quick glimpse of Mal and Star inside the nightlife world.

A cigarette ember or flash of smoke becomes the transition point. The first Polaroid freeze can hit here, turning the club moment into an image.

### 01:22.960-01:26.620 - VIP Crowd / Ribs Cue

The club becomes more specific: women, close bodies, table food, hands, jewelry, and flashes of faces opening up around Mal and Star.

This should feel like the dream version of the room: crowded, expensive, seductive, but still carrying the same dark-room undertone.

### 01:26.620-01:29.520 - Bottles / Fizz / Flash Frames

Bottles pop. Foam and liquid spray catch the light. Phone flashes and Polaroid-style freeze frames turn the party into a sequence of printed moments.

The club glamour should feel unstable, like each flash is already becoming evidence.

### 01:29.520-01:32.960 - Childhood Dream / Adult Status

The video crosscuts club, condo, and city movement. Mal's watch catches light. Blue hundreds sit on marble. Cigar smoke drifts through the terrace sunrise.

The images feel like childhood dreams turned into adult status objects, but every status object carries a shadow.

### 01:32.960-01:36.180 - Gold / Watch / Oil Slick

The status details become tactile: gold, watch face, jewelry, robe fabric, dark reflective surfaces, oil-slick color.

The luxury palette starts to bleed into the trap-house palette, making the present and past feel made from the same material.

### 01:36.180-01:39.580 - Bridge Threat / Ominous City

The Williamsburg Bridge enters as a darker city image. The bridge steel, train movement, and skyline take on an ominous threat.

Do not show literal harm. Keep this as atmosphere: silhouettes, hard cuts, a dangerous mythic energy around the bridge, and a sense that the city itself remembers what happened.

### 01:39.580-01:42.980 - Coke Tears / Onion Image

Back in the table world, the image goes close and uncomfortable. Faces are strained, eyes water, hands hover around product and cash without instructional detail.

The onion-like tear image becomes sensory: eyes, fumes, harsh light, and the emotional cost of the room.

### 01:42.980-01:46.400 - Standing Too Long / Feet

The montage drops to feet and floor. Worn shoes, shifting weight, tired ankles, dirty tile, long hours.

The clean shoes from the bedroom echo against feet trapped in the grind.

### 01:46.400-01:49.740 - Body Wear / Massage Image

The standing motif becomes surreal. Feet, hands, pressure points, the body treated like a tool that has been used too long.

The edit rhythm should feel repetitive and physical, like labor turning into pain.

### 01:49.740-01:53.000 - Business As Routine

The room's work becomes mundane and automatic. Cash, table, door, eyes, reset.

Mal moves with professional calm, but the repetition makes it feel like a job he cannot leave.

### 01:53.000-01:55.980 - Upstate Memory / Learning The Flip

A colder upstate memory cuts in: a house, kitchen or basement table, older furniture, a mentor-like presence, low practical light.

Show the atmosphere of learning and survival without showing mechanics. The point is inheritance, not instruction.

### 01:55.980-01:59.340 - Burning Lip Skin / Physical Cost

The montage becomes intimate and uncomfortable: heat, smoke, cracked lips, a flinch, harsh bulb light.

This is the physical cost of the room. Keep it sensory and brief, not explanatory.

### 01:59.340-02:03.120 - Harpoons / Propellers / Water Danger

The water survival language returns as surreal imagery: propeller shadows, harpoon-like silhouettes, bodies moving through dark water, reflections cutting across faces.

The dark room begins to feel oceanic, like the trap-house memory and survival imagery are merging into one threat.

### 02:03.120-02:14.500 - Photo Lab / Images Developing

Star and Mal arrive at the film printer or photo lab. The dark-room title becomes literal: machines, counter, envelope, prints, red-toned development light, wet-looking photographic surfaces.

They open the envelope and look through the prints. At first, the photos feel normal: train windows, city, nightlife, motion blur, fragments of the day. Then the images become wrong. They show the dirty couch, the old table, the hanging bulb, Mal under the dark-room light, younger shadows, and places they were not just standing in.

### 02:14.500-02:19.620 - Final Hook / Prints Become Portals

The final hook begins with the photos acting like portals. A printed image of the dark room starts moving.

Mal under the bulb, Star and Mal in the lab, and the luxury apartment begin cutting together with clarity. The photos are no longer records; they are active entrances into the memory.

### 02:19.620-02:23.120 - Table Scene Inside The Prints

The photo stack reveals the table world again: bodies around the room, work on the surface, the couch, the stained glass, the bulb.

Each photo briefly animates before freezing back into a print. The past keeps developing in their hands.

### 02:23.120-02:25.800 - Daylight Print Turns Dark

A photo with daylight in it darkens in Star and Mal's hands. The blinds image returns, but now as a printed artifact.

The same beam of light from earlier gets swallowed inside the photograph, proving the darkness follows the image across formats.

### 02:25.800-02:29.920 - Time Loop / Polaroid Evidence

Polaroid-style freezes accelerate. Club, condo, trap room, bridge, photo lab, and overhead bulb all become physical images.

The stack starts to feel like evidence from multiple timelines, all pointing back to the same room.

### 02:29.920-02:39.800 - Outro / Final Image That Sticks

The outro crosscuts all three worlds: Mal in the luxury apartment, Mal under the hanging bulb, Star and Mal on the train, Star and Mal in the photo lab, the dirty room appearing inside the clean apartment.

The final image should land as a loop. Option one: Star and Mal hold a photo of Mal under the bulb, look up, and the same bulb is above them. Option two: Mal leaves the apartment, but the sliced money-cake remains on the marble beside a Polaroid of the dark room. Either ending says the same thing: success does not erase the dark room; it only gives the past cleaner surfaces to develop on.
